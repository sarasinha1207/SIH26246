Forecast API

The same numbers as the dashboard, as plain JSON.

The API is read-only and open: no key and no login. It serves the 2026 to 2030 forecasts, the early-warning alerts, the Demand Index, the capacity gap, the severity ranking and seat scenarios, with the same filters as the dashboard: state, district, sector and trade.

Every response says that the data is simulated. It behaves like official statistics but describes no real district, and it is not an official statistic.

  • 7endpoints
  • 4filters
  • 60months ahead
  • 60requests a minute

Endpoints

All are GET requests on this site and return JSON. Add the filters to the address, for example /api/forecast?state=Kerala&sector=Healthcare.

Parameters

Values are checked against the lists in /api/meta; unknown or repeated parameters are refused with a clear message.

ParameterValues
stateOne of the ten state names, for example Kerala. Not case sensitive.
districtThe official LGD code, for example 118 for Agra, or the district name. A district fixes the state.
sectorOne of the five sectors, for example IT-ITeS.
tradeA trade code such as HLT-2221 (nursing professionals) or its full name. A trade fixes the sector.
measureE (employed workers), V (job vacancies), S (registered job seekers), several separated by commas, or all (the default).
horizonMonths ahead from 1 to 60, several separated by commas, or all. The default is 12, 36 and 60. For flags: 12, 36 or 60.
detailaggregate (the default: one total for the filters) or series (every district and trade series, with limit up to 200 and offset).
seat_change, from_year, targetFor the scenario: seat_change is a percentage from -90 to 500 applied to the seats from from_year on (default 0), and target is the certified per opening to plan for (0.2 to 5, default 1). The certification rate and the forecast openings are unchanged; order may also be needed.
level, order, yearFor severity: level is trade (the default), state, state_trade, district, district_sector or series (needs a state or a district); order is under (most undersupplied first, the default), over or severity (largest gap either way); year is one year from 2026 to 2030 (the default is 2030). For the capacity gap, year may be several years.
typeFor flags: shortage, oversupply or all.
changeFor flags: entering or leaving lists the series that enter or leave an alert tail between now (December 2025, actual) and the horizon of 12, 36 or 60 months, with the percentile now and then and the first month in the tail.
from, to, resolutionFor the Demand Index: months like 2026-12, and annual (December of each year, the default) or monthly.

Reading the numbers

Two ranges

The usual range was calibrated on 2010 to 2019; the wide range also uses the shock years 2020 to 2025. For totals of many series, such as a state, read the wide range: errors of neighbouring series move together.

Where there is no range

A range exists for India, a sector, a trade, a state, a state with a sector, a district with a sector, and a district with a trade. For other combinations the field is null and a note says so.

Points add up, ranges do not

Point forecasts of the trades add up exactly to their sector, and sectors to states and India. Ranges are calibrated for each level separately.

Seats are held flat

The capacity gap uses the 2025 to 2026 sanctioned seats for every year to 2030, an assumption, because no seat plans are forecast.

Seekers are the least reliable

Job-seeker forecasts and the fifth year are the weakest. Each series also carries a confidence label for each twelve-month band.

Same data as the dashboard

The API reads the files the dashboard loads, so a figure there and a figure here are the same figure, to the nearest person.

Limits and errors

StatusWhen
200The request was understood. The body has "ok": true.
400A parameter is unknown, repeated or has an invalid value; the message says which.
404The filters are valid but match no series.
405The API accepts GET only.
429More than 60 requests a minute from one address, or the daily limit of the whole site. Wait a minute and try again.

Responses may be cached for up to an hour. The API sets no cookies, keeps no record of what you ask, and allows requests from any website, since the data is public and read-only.
