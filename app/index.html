Why this exists

Training seats are planned for a market that has already moved.

Built for officials, planners and training providers who decide where to open seats, run camps or look closer.

Counts look backwards

Official counts describe last month or last year. A seat opened today produces a worker a year or more from now, so planning needs to see what is coming, not only what has been.

One number hides the doubt

A single forecast with no range invites false confidence. Every forecast here carries a range and a confidence label, both earned in testing on months the model had not seen.

Districts differ

State averages hide the districts where openings and job seekers are drifting apart. Each of the 56 districts is forecast on its own and compared with its peers.

Features of this website.

Built to be read by people who are not statisticians, and to be checked by people who are.

Interactive district map

Colour all 56 districts by sector, measure and months ahead, and zoom into a state.

Forecasts with honest ranges

Every forecast shows the 90% range around it, in a chart you can read month by month.

Confidence labels

High, medium or low, earned in testing on months the model had not seen, so you know when to lean on a number.

Early-warning flags

See every district of a sector in order, and which are heading into the tightest or loosest tenth.

Accuracy you can check

Results against simple baselines and the best any forecast could do, with the weak spots stated.

Open data downloads

Forecast, range, baseline and flag files, with checksums, free to download.
