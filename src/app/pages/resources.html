Resources

The data behind the forecasts, and where each part of it came from.

Everything the forecasts are built from is listed on this page: the monthly district and trade panel, the district catalogue, the occupation list, the crosswalk that links them and the district boundaries used by the map. For each one you can see what it contains, how large it is, where it came from, and whether it is real, simulated or produced by the model.

No public source reports these counts by district and trade, so every count on this site is simulated to behave like official statistics. The codes, populations and boundaries are real. The forecasts are real model output on that simulated history.
