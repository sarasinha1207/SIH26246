Why this exists

Training seats are planned for a market that has already moved.

Built for officials, planners and training providers who decide where to open seats, run camps or look closer.

Counts look backwards

Official counts describe last month or last year. A seat opened today produces a worker a year or more from now, so planning needs to see what is coming, not only what has been.

One number hides the doubt

A single forecast with no range invites false confidence. Every forecast here carries a range and a confidence label, both earned in testing on months the model had not seen.

Districts differ

State averages hide the districts where openings and job seekers are drifting apart. Each of the 317 districts is forecast on its own, trade by trade, and compared with its peers.
